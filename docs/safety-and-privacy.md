# Safety and privacy

SehatSetu AI does not diagnose, prescribe medicines, or recommend dosages.

Emergency handling uses a fixed phrase list. A match tells the person to call 112 or seek urgent care and states that the app did not place a call. The list cannot rule an emergency out. Wording has not been signed off as a clinical validation.

Audio is accepted in memory for transcription and is not saved. The server does not log transcript text. Identifiers such as name, phone, and address are not requested. A reminder note is stored in browser local storage only after a consent checkbox, and the user can delete it.

Health passages are short notes linked to WHO, the National Health Authority, or the National Health Mission. They are general information. Eligibility for a scheme is never guaranteed. Facility cards are labelled DEMO. Appointment responses set `confirmed` to false.
